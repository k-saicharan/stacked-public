How your data works

On the phone first. Backend only if you say yes.

  • Always local: every pallet, note, and schedule stays on your device. The app works fully offline.
  • Optional share: on first open you’ll see one clear prompt. Tapping yes sends only shift totals (encrypted in transit over HTTPS) so the pilot can learn what helps the floor.
  • Never uploaded: notes text, your name, or this signup email do not go into the analytics table.
