## Full description
```text
Stacked is an intuitive productivity log tool built for warehouse pickers, packers, and loaders to track shift output and items per hour on their own terms.

Created to replace paper notes and general text apps, Stacked lets you record your completed pallets with a quick-tap numeric layout so you can log your work and get back to your tasks.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap the item count as you wrap and complete each pallet.
2. The app logs the pallet instantly and timestamps it.
3. Stacked auto-detects whether you are working a Morning, Evening, or Night shift based on the time.

TRACK YOUR HOURLY PACE
See how you are performing across the shift, day, week, and month.
• Pallet Count: Know exactly how many pallets you have completed.
• Items/Hour: View your live hourly pace (items divided by logged time span).
• History: Review previous shifts to verify your completed hours, track milestones, and maintain accurate logs.

BUILT FOR ROTATING SHIFTS
If you work a rotating pattern, Stacked has you covered. Set up your current and previous shifts, and the app auto-calculates your rotation (Morning -> Night -> Evening) so you can view your upcoming schedule.

DOCUMENT OPERATIONAL CONTEXT WITH NOTES
Add quick, timestamped text notes directly to your shift log (for example, "dock delay," "forklift queue," or "system down") to explain why a shift was slow.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• No Accounts: Open the app and use it immediately. No email address, username, or login required.
• Offline Default: All your logs, statistics, and notes are stored locally on your device.
• Optional Sync: You can opt-in to sync shift summaries (pallets, items, and hourly rate) anonymously to backup your stats. Your name, email, and text notes never leave your device.

KEY FEATURES
• Simple layout designed for fast, one-handed input on the warehouse floor.
• Automatic shift detection for Morning, Evening, and Night shifts, including midnight crossings.
• Daily, weekly, and monthly statistics dashboards.
• Deterministic shift schedule predictor that matches standard rotating cycles.
• Swipe-to-delete logs with double-confirmation to prevent accidental taps.
• Easy data export and import to move your records to a new phone.
```
